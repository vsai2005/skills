# Review Scope

Prioritize:

1. correctness/regression risk;
2. authentication, authorization, secret or data-loss boundaries;
3. public API/event/schema compatibility;
4. migration/deployment overlap and rollback;
5. architecture ownership/dependency direction;
6. test integrity and unverified paths;
7. obvious performance/resource regressions;
8. maintainability issues likely to spread.

Do not spend review budget on unrelated formatting or speculative rewrites.
