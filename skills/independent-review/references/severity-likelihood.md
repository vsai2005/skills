# Severity × Likelihood

Rank findings using both consequence and evidence that the problem can occur.

| Consequence | Likelihood | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| catastrophic/high | likely or demonstrated | critical |
| high | plausible | important |
| medium | likely | important |
| high | very unlikely/speculative | investigate, do not automatically block |
| low | likely | optional unless repository policy says otherwise |

A critical label requires more than an alarming hypothetical. Cite the path, contract and evidence.
